=================================

-----

ranking pipeline. Production Octopus was not modified.

Development and held-out protocol
----------------------------------
30 tasks were used for development: the first six tasks in each category.
20 tasks were held out: the final four tasks in each category.
The relative-threshold alpha was selected only on the development split.
Selected alpha: 0.80.

P95 7, and maximum 7.

P90 15, P95 16, and maximum 18.

P95 23, and maximum 27.

Interpretation
--------------
No tested dynamic selector improved the recall/context frontier over Fixed-K=4.
Bounded Max Gap is more context-efficient than Fixed-K=4, but loses required
capability recall and complete-task rate. The relative threshold and score-knee
selectors recover full required recall, but use substantially more context.

The result does not justify changing production Octopus parameters or selection
behavior. Fixed-K=4 remains the strongest practical comparison point in this
experiment. Max Gap remains useful as a compact adaptive baseline, but it has
not demonstrated an advantage over Fixed-K=4 at the required recall level.

Notable failure
---------------
single_010 required railway_tools_service_list. It ranked second, while
Bounded Max Gap selected only rank one. This is a selection failure, not a
dense-ranking failure. Fixed-K=2 and the tested alternative selectors recovered
the tool.
